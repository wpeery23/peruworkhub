Verified remote work in Peru & Latin America

Peru Remote Work Hub is a verification-first resource for US companies hiring remote workers in Latin America — especially Peru — and for the job seekers, freelancers, and employers on both sides of that hiring.

Every factual claim on this site is meant to trace to a cited source. Where we do not have a source, we say “Not confirmed” instead of guessing. See our methodology for what “verified” and “not confirmed” mean here.

New site, growing. This site is new. The directory now lists 47 verified profiles (evidence supports 47, not 50). The guides are filled in, with any still-unverified item marked “To be verified” or “Not confirmed” instead of guessed.

There are no fake reviews, no invented salary figures, no fake testimonials, and no affiliate links yet on this site.

Who this site is for

  • Job seekers in Peru and Latin America looking for legitimate remote work paid in USD.
  • US employers deciding how to hire compliantly in Peru, including EOR options.
  • Freelancers in Peru figuring out how to receive USD payments with the lowest total cost.
  • People planning a move — marrying, retiring, or starting a business in Peru — who need checklists grounded in verifiable sources.

How to use this site

Start with the Directory if you are looking for work, or with the EOR Comparison if you are an employer. Check the last-verified date and evidence links on every profile — facts change, and this site is built to show its work.
